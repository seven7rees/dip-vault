The graphic and video carry the current format from `DIP-VAULT-FORMAT-NOTE.txt` (repo root):
the creator fee splits in half (launcher / pot, author share 0); after the builder's cut of
new pot money, the rest splits in three equal piles (dip buys - coins kept / weekly holder
payout / locked liquidity); no burn anywhere; a 1% brake only inside the first 10 minutes
of a hold.

The video is a labeled SIMULATION on an EXAMPLE LAUNCH. The counters are an illustrative
model (the builder's cut deducted before the thirds shown), not live results and not a
promise. Locking is framed as planned, not live. The pair is not audited.
