Subject: Dip Vault: the new format of the pair (for the graphic and the video)

Patrick,

Here is the Dip Vault pair as it is now built (2026-10-21), for the new flow graphic and the new video. Everything below is what the code does; the numbers marked "proposed" are yours to set.

THE ONE-LINE PITCH
No long sell tax. Hold through the week and get paid. The pot never burns: after graduation its coins go into the launch's locked liquidity.

THE BIG CHANGE FROM THE OLD PICTURE AND VIDEO
The old version burned the coins from dip buys. The new one keeps them. The pot's dip buys and its liquidity adds work together, the way LiquidityTek does it: dip buys catch the coin cheap, the liquidity steps pair those coins with SOL and add them to the launch's own locked LP. So the video's "dip buys + burns" frames become "dip buys, kept, then added as locked liquidity". Cut every mention of burning.

TRADES (the hook)
1. Buys: no extra cut from the hook. The launch's own creator fee and LP fee still apply, as on every coin.
2. Sells: a 1% brake only while the seller's hold clock is under 10 minutes. After 10 minutes the hook takes nothing on a sell. (The hook answers 0.8%; Bordrless adds a quarter on top, so about 1% trader-facing.)
3. The hold clock is a weighted-average entry time. Coins arriving in an empty wallet start the clock at now. Topping up moves the clock toward now in proportion to the amount. Sending coins out does not move the sender's clock. Emptying a wallet clears it.
4. The dust-delegate trick is addressed: the brake keys on the holding the coins really leave, not on whoever signs (tested on chain with a real delegate sale).

THE FEE
Every creator fee: 50% to the launcher, 50% to the pot. Author share 0 at the Workshop (we take nothing from the launcher's half); the builder's earning is a fifth of the pot, paid by the companion each step as LiquidityTek does. Not for the graphic.

THE POT, every step (every 5 minutes)
First the builder's cut: a fifth of new pot money (LiquidityTek's rate; leave it OFF the graphic and the video, the listing words carry it). Then the rest splits in three equal piles:

Pile 1, dip buys. Only when the price is 8% or more under the pot's own moving mark. At most 1% of the pool's real SOL per buy. At most one buy per 5 minutes. The coins bought are kept in the pot's own holding, never burned, never sold.

Pile 2, weekly holder payouts in SOL. Weeks start Thursday 00:00 UTC. A wallet counts in a week only if its clock started before that week began and it holds at least 1,000 coins; nothing before 6 hours of holding. Weights: 1x from 6 hours, 1.5x from 7 days, 2x from 30 days, plus 10% more if the wallet never sold into the pool since it was funded. Week one measures; from week two on, the holders the keeper reaches are paid by weight from the week's budget while it lasts, one holder per step (every 5 minutes), with no claim to make. Payouts under 0.0001 SOL are not sent.

Pile 3, liquidity. After graduation, about every 10 minutes, 1/24 of this pile pairs the coins the pot holds with SOL and adds them to the coin's own pool. The LP lands in the launch's own LP holding, which the launchpad does not spend. If the pot holds too few coins for the tranche, it buys the shortfall first.

Step senders earn up to 0.00002 SOL per step from the pot.

LIVE FIGURES ON THE COIN PAGE (new, via keeper.json "view")
Dip pile, Holder pile, Liquidity pile, Paid to holders, Coins held for LP, SOL into liquidity. These update within a second of the chain, so the video can show them as real counters.

WORDS TO KEEP OFF THE GRAPHIC
Burn, and the three words the Workshop brief forbids about a hook's safety. Use "not audited" where a disclaimer is needed.

SUGGESTED FRAME ORDER FOR THE VIDEO
1. A buy: no extra cut.
2. A sell inside 10 minutes: 1% brake. A sell after: no brake.
3. The fee splits 50/50, launcher and pot.
4. The pot splits into three piles (no builder-cut frame).
5. A dip: the pot buys and keeps the coins.
6. Graduation: the coins pair with SOL into the launch's locked liquidity.
7. A week passes: holders paid in SOL by weight.
Keep the "SIMULATION, EXAMPLE LAUNCH" label on every frame.

STATUS
