## Longer description (a post or the item page)

No long sell tax. Hold through the week and get paid.

Most hooks tax you for selling. Dip Vault takes 1% of a sell only in the first 10 minutes of a hold, to slow dumpers, and nothing after. Buys get no extra cut from the hook. The launch's own creator fee and LP fee apply as on every coin.

The creator fee: half goes to the launcher, all of it, no skim to the builder. Half goes to the pot.

The pot, every 5 minutes. First the builder's cut, a fifth of new pot money. The rest in three equal piles:
- Dip buys: when the price is 8% or more under the pot's own moving mark, at most 1% of the pool's SOL per buy, one buy per 5 minutes. The coins are kept, never burned, never sold.
- Weekly SOL to holders: a wallet counts in a week if it held at least 1,000 coins from before the week began (weeks start Thursday 00:00 UTC) and for at least 6 hours. Bigger and older holds earn more: 1x from 6 hours, 1.5x from 7 days, 2x from 30 days, 10% more if the wallet never sold into the pool. The keeper pays holders one a step by weight from the week's budget while it lasts. Nothing to claim.
- Liquidity: after graduation, about every 10 minutes, 1/24 of the pile pairs the coins the pot holds with SOL and adds them to the coin's own pool. The LP lands in the launch's own LP holding, which the launchpad does not spend.

Whoever sends a step is paid up to 0.00002 SOL from the pot. Self-reviewed, not audited. Nothing is promised. Bordrless fees apply.
