---

No early-sell tax. Hold 6 hours and get paid.

Most hooks tax you for selling. Dip Vault doesn't. Sell any time. The only fee from the hook is 1% in the first 10 minutes, to slow dumpers. After that, no sell cut. Buys: no cut from the hook.

What the fee does: half goes to the launcher - all of it, no skim to the builder. Half goes to a pot. The launcher sets the creator fee.

What the pot does (after the builder cut, the rest splits in thirds):
- 30% buys dips and burns what it buys. It buys when the price is 8%+ under its average, never more than 1% of the pool's SOL at once, one buy per 5 minutes.
- 30% is paid to holders in SOL every week. Payouts start after 6 hours of holding. Sell before that and you miss the payout. Bigger, older holds earn more: 1x at 6 hours, 1.5x at 7 days, 2x at 30 days.
- 30% buys the coin and locks it as permanent liquidity, a little at a time.

No fine print: the builder takes 1% to 10% of the pot, more as the pool grows. Whoever runs each step is paid up to 0.00002 SOL from the pot. Payout limits can change. Self-reviewed, not audited. Nothing is promised. Bordrless fees apply.
