No early-sell tax: hold 6 hours and get paid. Buys pay 0%. A sell pays a 1% brake only in its first 10 minutes of holding, then nothing. Half of every creator fee goes to the launcher, half to the pot. The pot splits in three: dip buys that burn the coin (only 8%+ under its own mark, at most 1% of the pool per buy, once per 5 minutes), weekly SOL to holders (6h+ held: 1x at 6h, 1.5x at 7d, 2x at 30d, 10% more if never sold), and liquidity locked for ever after graduation. The builder takes 1% to 10% of new pot money, rising with pool depth. Step senders earn up to 0.00002 SOL. Not audited.
