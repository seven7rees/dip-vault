A buyback for your coin that waits for real dips. Trade fees fill a pot. Every 5 minutes it checks the price. When the price is 8% under DIP's own mark (it follows the price up), the pot buys your coin and burns it: 1/48 of what is left in the pot (at least 0.0005 SOL), up to 4 times that on a 32% drop, never over 1% of the pool's SOL. Runs itself. Each creator fee splits: half to the pot, half to you. The builder takes up to 10% of new pot money. Step senders earn up to 0.00002 SOL. Launch with no hook or token rules. Not audited. Payout limits can change.
